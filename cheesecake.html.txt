<h1>Cheesecake</h1>
<p>Cheesecake is a rich, creamy dessert that melts in your mouth with every bite. Known for its smooth texture and lightly tangy-sweet flavor, this dessert is a favorite for all occasions—from celebrations to quiet evenings. With a buttery biscuit base and a luscious cream cheese filling, each slice offers a perfect balance of softness and crunch. Whether served plain or topped with fruits, cheesecake always feels indulgent and comforting.</p>
<img src="https://cdn.freecodecamp.org/curriculum/labs/recipe.jpg" alt="Cheesecake" width="600">



<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul>
  <li>1½ cups crushed digestive biscuits</li>
  <li>¼ cup melted unsalted butter</li>
  <li>2 tablespoons sugar</li>
  <li>2 cups (about 450g) cream cheese, softened</li>
  <li>1 cup powdered sugar</li>
  <li>1 teaspoon vanilla extract</li>
  <li>2 large eggs</li>
  <li>½ cup sour cream or fresh cream</li>
  <li>1 tablespoon all-purpose flour</li>
  <li>Fresh strawberries, blueberries, or other fruits</li>

</ul>


<h2>Instructions</h2>
<ol>
  <li>Preheat your oven to 160°C (320°F).</li>
  <li>Prepare the crust:

Crush the biscuits into fine crumbs.

Mix them with melted butter and sugar.

Press this mixture firmly into the bottom of a baking pan to make the base.

Bake for 10 minutes, then set aside to cool.</li>
  <li>Make the filling:

In a large bowl, mix cream cheese and sugar until smooth.

Add vanilla extract and mix again.

Add eggs one by one, mixing gently after each.

Add sour cream and flour, and stir until everything is smooth.</li>
  <li>Pour the filling over the baked crust.</li>
  <li>Bake the cheesecake for 45–50 minutes, or until the edges look set and the center is slightly wobbly.</li>
  <li>Cool down: Let the cheesecake cool to room temperature.</li>
  <li>Chill in fridge: Put it in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours (overnight is best) to set properly.</li>
  <li>Add toppings like fruits or syrup before serving.</li>
 

</ol>
